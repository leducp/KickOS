# M8.12 -- the M8 exit measurement, frozen

The measurement M9 is sized from. `roadmap.md`'s M8 section freezes two and rules they are not
interchangeable: M8.7 is the PRE-optimisation baseline and this is the EXIT one, taken after
M8.8 through M8.11 landed. Tree `b356b18d`, every capture's banner carrying that stamp and no
`-dirty`.

## The instrument is M8.7's, and that is checked rather than asserted

The figures below are read out of the captures by `.session/m812/figs.py`. Run over the M8.7
captures it reproduces **every published cell of that campaign's own table** -- all six
emulator presets, all four columns, and the four-core `lock-wait`, `doorbell`, `e2e-local` and
`e2e-cross` extras. So the two tables come out of one reader and a delta between them is the
kernel's, not the reader's. That check is the only thing separating this record from two
numbers taken different ways, and it is cheap: the M8.7 captures are still on the box.

**THE HEADLINE STATISTIC IS A PER-PRESET PROPERTY AND MIXING TWO OF THEM IS SILENT.**
`xmc4800-relax` builds with the min headline and prints `min/avg/max` where every other board
prints `p50/p99/max`. A reader taking field one from each would compare that board's MIN
against everyone else's `p50` and see a saving that is a label. The reader reads the label and
this record carries it in every XMC cell.

```

```

`switch` is UNCHANGED on all three silicon boards and on `qemu-x86_64`, and moves by at most
one bucket on the emulators. `p50` is a bucket low edge at an eighth of an octave, so
1536 to 1664 and 240 to 256 are one bucket and that row is flat within the instrument's
resolution. **The switch was not what M8.8 through M8.11 set out to move**, and it did not.

What moved is the lock and the round trip. `lock-hold` falls 15 to 27 percent on silicon and 31
to 67 percent on every emulator; the round trip falls 8 to 16 percent on silicon, 21 to 41
percent on the one-core emulators, and **77 to 79 percent on both four-core boards**. The
four-core collapse is the headline of this campaign: it is where removing a syscall from each
exchange and taking the reschedule out of the lock compound, and it is the figure M9 is sized
against.

## What these figures may NOT be used for

Everything M8.7's record forbids still holds and is not restated. Added by this pass:

- **No four-core figure is a kernel cost**, and the 77 to 79 percent round-trip fall is a
  reading of this emulator under this workload. There is no multi-core silicon on this bench,
  so nothing here separates the kernel's share from the emulator's.
- **The per-switch ABSOLUTE on the two ARM silicon boards moved for an instrument reason.**
  `MPU_COMMIT` is bracketed from M8.9 and reads 189 cycles on `f411disco`, and the switch
  distribution's bracket closes before the commit runs. So the switch row is like for like and
  the per-switch TOTAL is not, and it is not comparable with any ARM figure this project
  published before M8.9.
- **Nothing here prices address-space tagging, in either direction.** The residency lookup
  tagging put on the root-change path is a linear scan bounded by the live space count, 11 + 6
  per live space out of the linked arm64 image, and `qemu-arm64-bench` never changes the root,
  so `arch_aspace_activate` is not reached and every switch row reads the same with tagging and
  without. A reader looking for what tagging cost will not find it here. And the only vehicle on
  this bench that could measure it would report a loss: under `-icount shift=0` the flush the
  tag removes is worth about one instruction and the scan that replaces it costs a hundred,
  where on silicon the flush is a whole working set's refill traffic.
- **The XMC contributes wall clock only for its cycle rows' worth.** Three of four captures
  came back with a dead DWT; the fourth carries a live counter and is the one the cycle cells
  are read from. The wall-clock rows are valid in all four, the DWT not being in that path.

## The XMC4800's counter, and that a retry is what got one

Its DWT was dead across the first three attempts of this pass, `21 sampled row(s), 6 dead, 0
moving` each time, and the chain refused all three. The fourth came back `0 dead, 12 moving`.
That is this board's documented character rather than a regression, and it is worth recording
that M8.11 gave up after two attempts and had to cite a pre-review tree: **the cure was more
attempts, not a different tree.**

The silicon captures cost bench time, which is the test `docs/README.md` states for what
this directory holds. Six are embedded below; the boot-ROM preamble ahead of the last
banner box is dropped and nothing else is, as M8.7 did. The hash in the manifest is over
the file as the instrument wrote it, carriage returns and boot-ROM bytes included, and is
what says the embedded text was derived from it. The XMC pair is its LIVE-counter capture
and one of its dead ones, that board's intermittency being a reading to keep rather than a
failure to hide.
