# M10.2 exit measurement

The tree M10.2 closes on, "Record the q35 switch-cost limit and keep the FS base WRMSR", built
with the KickOS toolchain alone: every CI job, the fleet's silicon on the bench, the red zones and
the memory budgets of every preset, the bench rows against the M10.1 exit, and the q35 switch rows
under KVM against decision 15's limit (`docs/design-m10-toolchain.md`). The comparison tree is the
M10.1 exit tree, "Record M10.1's exit measurement", built with the vendor toolchains it shipped
with. The [raw captures](M10.2_exit_captures) are the whole serial output of each silicon and KVM
run, archived as M10.1's were, CRLF normalised to LF and each byte outside printable ASCII written
as `?`; [`M10.2_exit_captures.sha256`](M10.2_exit_captures.sha256) digests them.

## The package

Every family was built from the recipe this tree exports, in a fresh Conan store, two at a time
on eight cores, with `conan create conan/toolchain --name kickos-toolchain-<family>
--build=missing` as CI's toolchain jobs run it and the pinned sources taken from
`KICKOS_TOOLCHAIN_SOURCES`. The store was
then indexed with `tools/kickos-toolchain.sh`, as a user provisions it, with
`KICKOS_TOOLCHAIN_NO_RELEASE` and `KICKOS_TOOLCHAIN_NO_BUILD` set so that nothing but these
packages could serve, and every build below names that one folder through `KICKOS_TOOLCHAIN`.
`tools/kickos-toolchain-release.sh` then assembled the release folder, one `conan cache save
--no-source` archive per family beside the pinned sources and a `SHA256SUMS`, for the maintainer
to upload under `toolchain-1.0`.

Every family is GCC 16.2.0 at recipe revision `1b4d7ee001a5b90f6e9c1e0a4fb26f2a`, package id
`63fead0844576fc02943e16909f08fcdddd6f44b` for Linux x86_64. Each family's link check, the recipe's
own C++ program linked full and, on Arm, nano for every multilib the recipe names, ran again on the
index and links: Arm 4 of 4, riscv64 2 of 2, the other four 1 of 1.

xmc4800-relax default list's endpoint cascade, and f302nucleo's four images all pass, and so does
the rx72m uartirq capture, whose fault record landed on a line boundary here, so the re-join the
TAP check now carries was not needed. f302nucleo's skips and partials are the ones its manifest
declares, 5, 3, 8 and 2 skips over the four images. Its banner is the terse format, `c faf82af9`,

cycle rows; the wall-clock rows below stand.

## The bench against the M10.1 exit

Each cell reads M10.1 / M10.2 (change), each side the median of three captures, and every row of
this tree's captures repeated across the three to the nanosecond. The XMC4800, F411 and ESP32-C6 are
set against the [M10.1 exit's own captures](M10.1_exit_captures). The M10.1 exit has none of the
RX72M or the ESP32, so their M10.1 side is the M10.1 exit tree built with the vendor toolchains it
shipped with, GNURX 14.2.0.202511 and esp-16.1.0, and captured the same day as this record
(`base-` in the captures).

**On both Cortex-M boards a spawn got 6.7 to 8.0 percent cheaper, and a payload copy a third.**
The interrupt copy rows, `wcase-irq` at 64 to 1,024 bytes, are 31 to 36 percent faster on the
F411, 31 to 40 on the ESP32-C6, 19 to 25 on the ESP32 and 30 to 38 on the RX72M, the copy loops
keeping their bounds in registers; call/reply at 256 bytes is 1.3 to 1.4 percent faster on both
Arm boards, 4.8 on the ESP32-C6 and 7.9 on the ESP32.

**Two rows moved the other way.**
- The ESP32-C6's spawn/exit is 7.3 and 6.8 percent slower, and its ping-pong 1.7. The preliminary
  pass of the same day, on the same riscv64 recipe, read spawn/exit +0.8 percent against M10.1:
  the 6.5 percent between the two lies in tree changes between them, not attributed here.
- The RX72M's `switch` p50 reads 160 cycles against GNURX 14.2's 128, 666 ns against 533, in
  every capture, while its ping-pong per switch is 0.1 to 0.6 percent faster.

## The q35 switch under KVM

Decision 15's limit, ruled 2026-10-01: the whole switch window, `switch:` and `switch-vec:`
alike, at most 700 core cycles at p50 under KVM, each run's window converted at that run's own
cycles-per-tick ratio. qemu-x86_64-bench of this tree, with the split's instrumentation applied as
5.3 prescribes, the dependent-add clock probe and the exact count of every value below 1,023
ticks printed beside each switch row, booted twenty times on QEMU 11.1.1 with `-accel kvm`, ten
with `-cpu host` and ten with the preset's model, interleaved, pinned with `taskset` to cores 2 and
3, the fast threads of two physical cores, with no other heavy work on the host.

| Model | Row | Rows | p50 / p99, ticks | Cycles per tick | p50, core cycles, median / worst row | Mean, core cycles | Limit |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- | ---: | --- |
| `-cpu host` | `switch:` | 60 | 240 / 260 | 2.57 | 618 / 668 | 641 | pass |
| `-cpu host` | `switch-vec:` | 20 | 240 / 260 | 2.58 | 618 / 618 | 633 | pass |
| preset | `switch:` | 60 | 240 / 260 | 2.57 | 618 / 668 | 640 | pass |
| preset | `switch-vec:` | 20 | 240 / 260 | 2.57 | 618 / 618 | 633 | pass |

The window reads 618 core cycles at p50, 82 under the limit, and its mean of 641 is the 642 the
ruling measured. The worst single row, 668, is still under it. The dirty variant and the preset's
model read as the clean row and `-cpu host`.

## Red zones and memory budgets

Every preset `tests/static/trap_redzone_roots.txt` lists, 89, built and walked on this tree;
87 on the comparison tree, which cannot build esp32-wroom-benchsmp, its errnoprobe overflowing
IRAM with esp-16.1.0, and has no qemu-x86_64-smp2root1. No class on this tree measures more than
it reserves.

**Zero slack, measured equal to the reserve:**
- PREEMPT 432 of 432 on esp32-wroom, -st and -bench, as on the comparison tree.
- esp32c6-wroom-amp2-n0's EXITK and RET, 640 of 640, and SYSPRIV, 832 of 832, as on the
  comparison tree.
- EXITK 1232 of 1232 on qemu-arm64-benchgicv3 and qemu-arm64-benchsmp12.
- q35: SYSK 1800 of 1800 on qemu-x86_64 and qemu-x86_64-bench, IRQ, IRQK and IST 576 of 576 on
  qemu-x86_64-bench, and SYSPRIV and SYSPRIVSW 2304 of 2304 on qemu-x86_64-smp12.
- IDLE 64 of 64 on every esp32-wroom preset, as on the comparison tree.

**What moved.** q35's classes are 56 to 232 bytes deeper, the vector state and newlib (5.3). Most
of their reserves rose to cover it; the one-core SYSK reserve and the twelve-core SYSPRIV one did
not, and read zero slack.
AArch64's SYSK reserve is 2048 and rv64's EXITK and SYSK 2048, and the comparison tree on its
vendor toolchains already measured above its own reserves on qemu-arm64-benchgicv3 and
-benchsmp12, FAULT 1232 over 1216 and SYSK 1840 over 1808, and on qemu-riscv64-benchsmp and
-benchsmp2, SYSK 1936 over 1904 and EXITK 1136 over 1120. Most of armv8a's other classes are 16
to 48 bytes shallower, a few SYSK and PANIC figures 16 deeper. Elsewhere the moves are 4 to 20
bytes: esp32-wroom-smp and -benchsmp PREEMPT measure 576 and 592 against a reserve of 640, and
f411disco-bench SVCK 768 against 832. `redzones.tsv` in the captures lists every class of every
preset on both trees.

**Memory.** The tightest budget, among regions above 1 KiB, is still the 64 KiB flash of the
F103 and F302 selftest images: bluepill-c8-st's `selftest_p4` has 1,120 bytes left against 1,108,
f302nucleo-st's 1,196 against 1,224, and bluepill-c8's and f302nucleo's `selftest` about 4 KiB.
esp32-wroom-benchsmp's errnoprobe has 5,672 bytes of IRAM left; the other lx6 presets gained 6 to
10 KiB of IRAM. No region is over. armv8a's 20 KiB boot region is full by construction on both
trees, the boot code and the page-aligned identity tables filling it.
