# M8.8 -- the per-switch and per-wake plumbing, on silicon

The first attribution pass against the M8.7 baseline. Every figure below is a delta against
`docs/archive/M8.7_rebaseline_meas.md`, and nothing here replaces that baseline: M8.9 through
M8.11 are attributed to it too, and M8.12 is the M8-exit measurement M9 is judged against.

**THE EVIDENCE IS THIS FILE.** The four silicon captures are below, whole, one section each, and
every row in every table is read off them. The `sha256` of each ORIGINAL is in the manifest at the
end, so a reader holding one can identify it.

## What was done to the bytes, and what that claim actually is

Two things, and nothing else: the carriage returns are stripped, and a prefix ahead of the banner
box is dropped. **The claim was verified rather than asserted.** Each embedded text was put back
through the transformation in reverse -- the dropped prefix restored from the original's own
leading bytes, a carriage return put back before every newline -- and compared with the original;
all four reconstruct BYTE FOR BYTE. So nothing inside a capture is touched, and the only thing the
wording has to get right is what the dropped prefix is. It differs by board:

- On both `f411disco` captures the prefix is EXACTLY three bytes: the single non-ASCII framing
  byte a console cable emits as the reader arms, and the CRLF that ends its line. It sits ahead of
  the first banner, nothing else precedes that banner, and neither file holds another non-ASCII
  byte.
- On both `esp32c6-wroom` captures the prefix is everything ahead of the LAST banner box -- 528
  bytes on `m88vC1`, 683 on `m88vC2`. That is the boot-ROM preamble, and ahead of the preamble a
  truncated fragment of the same reset's first, aborted boot. **"The non-ASCII framing byte ahead
  of the first banner" is NOT a true description of this pair and is not claimed for it**:
  `m88vC1`'s one non-ASCII byte sits inside the fragment, mid-line and behind a partial banner,
  and `m88vC2` holds no non-ASCII byte anywhere.

## The tree these were taken on, and what it excludes

**The tree is named by what it carries, not by a hash.** It carries SM-5, PERF-4, PERF-1 and
PERF-5, and it does **not** carry PERF-7 or P2, both of which landed after the capture. That list
is what a reader decides a figure against: **no figure in this record covers PERF-7 or P2.**

Neither later item can move a cycle figure on these two boards, and the reason is what the two
bench presets compile rather than a judgement about the items. Both presets set
`CONFIG_MEMORY_MODEL_MPU=y`, so `KICKOS_HAVE_MPU` is 1 in both, and both are one-core images.
What P2 leaves in the tree is the `#else` arm of `MpuSet::apply`, reached only where
`KICKOS_HAVE_MPU` is 0; a `static_assert` bound tightened from `<= 32` to `< 32`, which emits
nothing; and a blank line. Its region-board half, the same-set skip, landed after the capture and
was reverted, so no tree either side of the capture carries it. PERF-7 hoists reads of
`kickos_kernel_core()`, which at one core is a macro expanding to the literal `0u`, and its
remaining edits sit behind `#if KICKOS_KERNEL_CORES > 1`. What survives on these boards is an
argument carrying that constant into a switch helper: layout at the relink floor's scale, and not
work.

The exact source tree is recoverable from the annotated tag `m8.8-meas-source`. **A tag reaches a
later reader only if it was pushed, so the content above is the provenance and the tag is the
convenience.** The commit it names, `2bbea107`, is not in the squashed history and resolves to
nothing there.

Both boards ran `mpu enforce`, tickless, telemetry off, two flash-and-capture cycles each. Within
one board the two logs are byte-identical apart from the build stamp -- every cycle figure, every
nanosecond and every sample count repeats exactly across separate flashes -- which is the same
determinism the M8.7 campaign recorded and the reason two captures a board is enough.

## The headline: one backend class gave up a whole timer disarm, the other gave up two stores

`KTIME_REARM` is the row the milestone was aimed at, and the two boards were chosen so that one
of them could contradict the other:

| board | M8.7 | M8.8 | delta |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `f411disco` (armv7m, 84 MHz) | 93 | 59 | -34 |
| `esp32c6-wroom` (rv32imac, 160 MHz) | 61 | 56 | -5 |

The clamping SysTick gives up a whole timer disarm, two strongly-ordered PPB writes; the absolute
CLINT comparator gives up two posted stores. **The sevenfold gap between the two is the result,
not the 34.** One backend class may not be quoted for the other, and a record that published only
the armv7m figure would be publishing the wrong number.

These are the raw rows as the instrument prints them. The corrected leaf is `leaf - NULL`, and
`NULL` reads 1 on `f411disco` and 2 on `esp32c6-wroom`, so corrected the move is 92 to 58 and 59
to 54. The correction does not change the gap.

## The limits that bound every figure here

- **`p50` and `p99` are log-linear histogram BUCKET LOW EDGES**, eight buckets per octave, while
  `min`, `avg` and `max` come from the exact accumulator. So `lock-hold`'s p50 falling by "exactly
  192" on both boards is three buckets on each and not a physical agreement, and the p99 deltas
  duly disagree: -128 on `f411disco` against -64 on `esp32c6-wroom`. **A percentile delta equal on
  two boards is a bucket artifact until an exact row says otherwise.**
- **`avg == min` over a large `n` means the `max` is a SINGLE SAMPLE.** Most phase rows here read
  that way on both trees. A max differenced against a max is one sample against one sample.
- **A deterministic RELINK FLOOR of one to four cycles sits on small rows.** It is measurable on
  bodies whose source did not change -- `CALL_RESOLVE` +1 and +3, `CALL_MINT_CAP` +1 and +4,
  `SWITCH_BOOK` +1 and +2, `WAKE_UNPARK` +2 on both -- so a leaf delta at or under it is layout
  and not work.
- **Part of every PERF-1 row delta exists only in a bench build.** Each removed nested `IrqLock`
  also removes a lock-probe open/close pair, and the close stamps the cycle counter BEFORE the
  depth test, so a nested acquisition paid a counter read that bought nothing. Hand-counting puts
  the real share somewhat over half on armv7m; it cannot be pinned closer than about ten cycles
  until a calibration phase exists that sites an empty nested lock inside an enclosing span.
- **The `lock-hold` population is the workload's, not the fastpath's.** The published row comes
  from the throughput window and `tests/integration/check_bench_lock.sh` refuses any window
  holding a fastpath swap; `fastpath-swaps` reads zero in every window of all four captures.
- **The timer dedup's hit rate is a property of THIS workload.** The bench players are FIFO at
  quantum 0 against an empty sleep queue, so the next deadline is the sentinel on every switch by
  construction and the dedup hits essentially always. A round-robin mix arms a fresh slice at most
  switch-ins and hits far less. Any attribution must name the workload it quotes.
- **`e2e-local` is a swept row of fifty samples.** Its 99th nearest rank IS rank 50, so its `p99`
  is the maximum wearing a percentile's label. Neither tree's cell is worth differencing alone.

## What this record does NOT witness

- **`esp32c6-wroom`'s `lock-hold` max grew 2013 to 2186 and nothing accounts for it.** Bit-exact
  in all three windows of both captures on both trees, so six samples a side, and `n` is
  bit-identical -- the population and the lock structure are unchanged and one outermost window's
  body grew by 173 cycles. The candidates are refuted by object code: the deleted backend timer
  caches (this part never had one), the MMIO saving (five cycles on this board, measured, and the
  wrong sign), the four sites that gained an acquisition, and the relink floor, which is three
  times too small. Open in `TODO.md` with the layout-perturbation control that would settle it.
- **Two once-per-run excursions vanished from code this milestone did not touch.** On
  `esp32c6-wroom`, M8.7's `CALL_VALIDATE` read `261/2645` with `avg` equal to `min` over 220000
  samples, and `REPLY_TOTAL` carried a max of 4687 against children that do not sum to it; M8.8
  reads `164/164` and `1500/1938`. PERF-5 explains the `CALL_VALIDATE` AVERAGE and none of the
  max, and its negative control is `REPLY_VALIDATE`, the identical unchanged walk, which reads a
  constant over 260000 samples in both trees. A once-per-run excursion left unmodified code and
  wants a cause before either max is quoted.
- **P2's region-board value is a peripheral store and is UNSIZED.** It is measured only as 10
  instructions a switch down to 1 on a translating board, and no capture here was taken on a tree
  carrying it.
- **No shipped preset runs the bench workload under emulation with enforcement on.** So nothing
  in the fleet cross-checks these two silicon boards on the protection path, and the MPU rows
  above are what two parts said, not what the design costs.
