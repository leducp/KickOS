# M10.5.5: the plain app and the selftest on the smallest boards

**Captured 2026-10-04 against `M10.5` = d5e87777**, "Charge and bound the AMP user share's seat and
type". The measurement `docs/design-m10-fleet.md` section 3 asks for before any app on `microbit`,
`f302nucleo` and `bluepill-c8` moves onto a composition, with the per-board decision on what the
selftest as a task costs. Nothing here changes a defconfig; the decision below is for the selftest
row to apply.

## What was built, and how it was read

**The presets.** The plain app on `microbit`, `f302nucleo` and `bluepill-c8`; the selftest on
`f302nucleo-st` and `bluepill-c8-st`, and on `microbit`, which has no `-st` preset because its base
defconfig enables the selftest. Every preset as `cmake/presets/arm.json` states it (MinSizeRel,
the selftest images at `-Os`), with no knob overridden except where a row says so.

**The toolchain** is the KickOS Arm family at GCC 16.2.0, served by the index at
`/var/tmp/kickos-archgaps-tc` through `KICKOS_TOOLCHAIN`: nano newlib on `microbit`'s armv6m, full
newlib on the two armv7m parts.

**The tree** is a `git archive` of the commit above, so the commit label is `nogit` in every image
and the stamps have one length. A scratch app and the selftest's variants were added to that copy
only; nothing of them is in the tree.

**The three images.** One plain C `main` that prints a line and returns 5:

| image | links | `main` runs as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A | the `kickos` leaf | root, under the default init |
| B | the `kickos_cxx` leaf | root, under the default init |
| C | `KickOS::kernel` and `KickOS::system_default` | the `main` task of the board's default composition |

The selftest's A and B are each of its images on the two leaves, with the image's own definitions,
include path and options. Its C links `KickOS::kernel` and the system target of the composition
section 4.4 describes, written for this measurement and linked for size only; the arms were not
moved off root. The `heap` is each preset's `KICKOS_USER_HEAP_SIZE` and the `stack` its
`KICKOS_ROOT_STACK_SIZE`; `accepts` is the board default's:

```
version: 1
board: f302nucleo            # bluepill-c8: heap 0, stack 2048; microbit: heap 1024, stack 2048,
stdout: kernel               #   accepts [no_protection, no_privilege_split]
ends: main
heap: 0
accepts: [no_protection]
tasks:
  - name: main
    entry: kickos_main
    stack: 1536
    priority: 31             # KICKOS_PRIO_MAX on all three builds
    authority: [memory, system, pinmux, irq, console, tasks]
```

All three admit. `kickos_compose cost` gives the same figures for it and for each board's default:
4 capability slots, 1 notification, 1 thread, 1 task, 2 domains (the two the kernel keeps), 1 self
grant, and 1 reservation (2 on `f302nucleo`).

**The instrument** is `size -A` over each linked ELF, each section classed by its `readelf -S`
flags: flash is `.text` (which holds `.rodata` on these scripts), the other read-only loaded
sections (`.ARM.exidx`, `.ARM.extab`, the init arrays) and `.data`'s load image; static RAM is
`.data`, `.bss` and `.userheap`. The arena is `__kickos_ram_end - __kickos_ram_start`; what the boot
and the init spend of it is replayed from `arch/common/boot_arena.ld.h` for A and B, and read from
the system target's `__kickos_system_arena_*` symbols for C, alignment run-ups included. B and C are
deltas against A built in the same directory from the same tree. An image whose flash overflows
was linked with `--noinhibit-exec` so it could be measured; the overflow is the figure reported, and
no such link hid a failed arena assert.

**The null control.** Every A image (the plain app on the three base presets and all fourteen
selftest images) was built a second time in a second build directory of the same path length. Each
pair has identical `size -A` sections and identical loaded bytes once `kickos_build_time`,
`kickos_build_commit` and `kickos_app_build_time` are masked; unmasked, each pair differs in 6
bytes, the stamps. So a delta below is the image's, not the instrument's.

**A split does not reach it.** The selftest built with every region elided, the fixed cost of an
image, is:

| preset | A | B | C | left for arms under C |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| microbit | 41244 | 52848 | 60392 | 201752 |
| f302nucleo-st | 38188 | 53248 | 61692 | 3844 |
| bluepill-c8-st | 38268 | 53024 | 61428 | 4108 |

Of the 23504 bytes C adds to that floor on `f302nucleo-st`, 15060 are B's: the exception runtime
(personality routine, unwinder, the exception tables of the selftest's own code) and the allocator
it pulls in. The rest is the init, the table and `kickos_main`. The arms of each region cost, under
C, the image less the floor: on `f302nucleo-st` 27048, 26440, 25584, 21684, 6712 and 7268 bytes,
114736 in all; on `bluepill-c8-st` 27080, 26448, 25592, 21708, 6728 and 7284, 114840. The smallest
region alone exceeds what an image leaves. Packed perfectly, the suite would take at least 30
images on `f302nucleo` and 28 on `bluepill-c8`, against 6 today, and only if no single arm costs
more than 3844 or 4108 bytes, which was not measured.

## Slots and width

| | microbit | f302nucleo(-st) | bluepill-c8(-st) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `KICKOS_MAX_THREADS` / `KICKOS_MAX_TASKS` | 4 / 6 | 2 / 4 | 2 / 4 |
| A, B: threads / tasks left to `main` | 4 / 4 | 2 / 2 | 2 / 2 |
| C: threads / tasks left to `main` | 3 / 3 | 1 / 1 | 1 / 1 |
| root's width, summed (`KICKOS_CAP_TABLE_SUPPLY`) | 10 (16) | 7 (7) | 7 (7) |
| the selftest's optional 3 granted to root | yes | no | no |
| `KICKOS_CAP_CHILD_WIDTH`, at `KICKOS_MAX_SPAWN_GRANTS` 6 | 7 | 7 | 7 |

The task pool seats idle's task and root's, so A leaves `KICKOS_MAX_TASKS` less 2; C adds `main`'s.
Root's thread is outside `KICKOS_MAX_THREADS` and `main`'s entry is inside it. A child's 7 slots
hold the 2 below `KICKOS_CAP_FIRST_DYNAMIC` and the selftest's peak of 5. On `microbit` root holds
the 3 optional slots too, so as a task the arm needing them would newly skip; on the STM32 parts
root's supply already withholds them and the width is unchanged. The init holds one notification,
leaving every board's pool at or above its per-task budget, and no extra domain.

## What each knob costs

Raised in a scratch build one at a time, then together, read on the A image `selftest`; the arena
asserts read on every A and C image.

| knob | microbit | f302nucleo-st | bluepill-c8-st |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `KICKOS_MAX_THREADS` + 1 | +1320 B .bss, and one 2048 B stack in the arena | +648 B .bss, and one 1024 B stack | +648 B .bss, and one 2048 B stack at 2048 alignment |
| `KICKOS_MAX_TASKS` + 1 | +24 B .bss | +24 B .bss | +24 B .bss |
| `KICKOS_MAX_SPAWN_GRANTS` 6 to 9 | +320 B .bss (child width 10, two 8-slot chunks), 48 B more staged on a spawner's stack | not needed | not needed |
| all of the above | +1728 B .bss; every A and C image passes every arena assert, the tightest (`selftest_p3`'s C) with 1376 B of static RAM to spare | +672 B .bss; every A image passes; the C images fail the chip's pool arena assert, short of static RAM by 352 (`selftest`), 416, 608, 256, 800 B, `_p6` alone passing | +672 B .bss; every A image passes; C `_p5` fails the pool arena assert, short of static RAM by 928 B, the other five pass with 352 to 1088 B to spare |

## The decision, per board

**`microbit`: raise, it fits.** In `boards/microbit/configs/base/defconfig`, which carries the
selftest: `KICKOS_MAX_THREADS` 4 to 5, `KICKOS_MAX_TASKS` 6 to 7, `KICKOS_MAX_SPAWN_GRANTS` 6 to 9.
That gives the task the 4 threads and 4 tasks root had and a child width of 10, the 2 reserved and
the peak of 5 and the optional 3, for 1728 bytes of .bss; the largest C image is 113476 bytes of the
256 KiB flash.

**`f302nucleo`: MISFIT.** The selftest as a task does not fit the 64 KiB flash at any split the
ruled extra region reaches: the largest image is over by 23204 bytes, all six are over (by 2868 at
the least), and the arm-less image under C is 61692 bytes, leaving 3844 for arms whose smallest
region costs 6712. On RAM, the thread raise also fails: `KICKOS_MAX_THREADS` 2 to 3 leaves five of
the six C images short of the pool arena assert by 256 to 800 bytes. `KICKOS_MAX_TASKS` 4 to 5 fits
at 24 bytes and the width needs no raise. Raised to the maintainer.

**`bluepill-c8`: MISFIT.** The same flash picture: the largest image over by 22972 bytes, all six
over (by 2620 at the least), the arm-less image under C 61428 bytes, leaving 4108 for arms whose
smallest region costs 6728. `KICKOS_MAX_THREADS` 2 to 3 fits five C images with 352 to 1088 bytes to
spare and leaves `selftest_p5` 928 bytes short; `KICKOS_MAX_TASKS` 4 to 5 fits at 24 bytes; the width needs no
raise. Raised to the maintainer.

The plain app moves on all three boards as it is: C fits, at 7692 to 8288 bytes of flash and 20 to
436 bytes of static RAM over A.
