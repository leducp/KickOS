# M8.13 -- the end-to-end addendum, beside the frozen table

**THE FROZEN FIGURES REMAIN M8.12'S READING OF M8.12'S TREE.** Not one cell of
[`M8.12_meas.md`](M8.12_meas.md) is touched here, and nothing below replaces one.
`roadmap.md` rules that nothing M8.13 lands may change a figure the exit measurement
published, and this is how that rule is kept while the delivery path inside the span
`BD_IRQ_E2E_LOCAL` and `BD_IRQ_E2E_CROSS` measure is rewritten: a NEW record, with its own
instrument, its own conditions and its own control.

## What moved under the span

M8.13 made the notification a first-class object. A raise no longer sets a bit in the
serving thread's TCB; it sets a bit in a `Notification` the thread is BOUND to, and an IRQ
line is one signaller among others rather than the only kind there is. The waiter's body
changed name and shape with it: `irq_wait_timed` is gone and `notify_wait` is what parks.
`KICKOS_BENCH_E2E_PARK_MARK()` moved with the park, under the same lock and still ahead of
the block, and `tests/static/check_bench_e2e_publish.sh` now reads that body by name --
loudly, because the reader refuses a body whose bracket it cannot see rather than reporting
a clean one. It PASSES on both four-core bench presets and reports 180 instructions in
`notify_wait` with the mark inside the bracket, so it is reading the real body.

## THE INSTRUMENT IS PAIRED, AND THAT IS NOT THE SHAPE M8.12 USED

M8.12 took five runs a preset on a quiet box -- load 4.2 to 5.5 throughout -- and compared
absolutes against M8.7. **This record could not do that, and says so rather than publishing
an absolute that is not one.** A sibling agent held this box at load 28 to 39 for the whole
window with its own 24-process load experiment, and a first attempt taken at load 31 read
`e2e-local` 20480 ns on `qemu-riscv-bench` against M8.12's 6656: three times the frozen
figure, and every nanosecond of it the box.

So the instrument is a PAIR. Each run of the M8.13 image is preceded by a run of the
**baseline image built from `885833b3`**, back to back, on one box, through one capture
harness, and the record carries the load average of each. A pair taken seconds apart shares
its contention; the delta between the two sides is the kernel's. The reader is M8.12's own
`figs.py` extractor -- the same regexes over the same rows -- with only the per-preset loop
replaced by the pairing.

**THE CONTROL IS THAT THE BASELINE SIDE REPRODUCES THE FROZEN TABLE.** Five of the six
published e2e cells come back exactly, at loads of 5.5 to 19:

| preset | row | M8.12 published | baseline side here |
|---|---|---|---|
| `qemu-riscv-bench` | `e2e-local` | 6656 | **6656** |
| `qemu-arm64-bench` | `e2e-local` | 8192 | **8192** |
| `qemu-arm64-benchsmp` | `e2e-local` | 73728 | 65536 (one bucket low) |
| `qemu-arm64-benchsmp` | `e2e-cross` | 114688 | **114688** |
| `qemu-riscv64-benchsmp` | `e2e-local` | 65536 | **65536** |
| `qemu-riscv64-benchsmp` | `e2e-cross` | 114688 | **114688** |

The one cell that does not is `qemu-arm64-benchsmp`'s `e2e-local`, one bucket below its
frozen value. Without that check the whole pairing would be two numbers taken two ways.

**FOUR OF THE SIX E2E CELLS DO NOT MOVE, AND THE TWO THAT DO MOVE BY EXACTLY ONE BUCKET.**
`p50` here is a bucket LOW EDGE at an eighth of an octave, which STATE.md's M8.12 section
already names as the reason the switch row reads unchanged: one bucket is this histogram's
resolution and not a measured quantity. `qemu-riscv-bench`'s `e2e-local` steps 6656 to 7168
and `qemu-riscv64-benchsmp`'s `e2e-cross` steps 114688 to 122880; both are one step.
**Neither is a claim that the path got slower, and neither is a claim that it did not.** What
this record supports is the narrower statement the rule actually needs: the rewrite did not
move the end-to-end span by a quantity this instrument can resolve.

## What this record does NOT say

- **It is not an absolute.** Every figure was taken at loads of 5.5 to 19 against M8.12's
  4.2 to 5.5. Only the paired DELTA is offered, and only because both sides of each pair
  share their contention.
- **The tip image is `-dirty`.** The work is uncommitted, so the M8.13 banner reads
  `885833b3-dirty` and the baseline image, exported from `885833b3` without a `.git`, carries
  no stamp at all. `campaign.sh`'s clean-tree refusal is therefore NOT the gate here; the
  paired harness records the banner it saw instead. A committed re-capture would be worth
  more than this one.
- **The two non-e2e presets of the frozen table are absent.** `qemu-riscv64-bench` and
  `qemu-x86_64-bench` carry an `e2e-local` row each and were not paired, the campaign being
  cut to what the window allowed.
- **`qemu-riscv-bench`'s non-e2e rows are the noisiest and were taken at the highest load**
  (19.0 down to 11.2, the first preset captured). Its `lock-hold` +30% and `rt8` +11% sit
  beside `+0%` on the three presets captured later at 5.5 to 11, which is what says they are
  the box rather than the kernel. The per-run load of every capture is in the harness output
  beside the body.
- **No silicon.** The bench was held by another agent for the whole window, so this is an
  emulator record only. The three silicon boards of M8.12's second half have no counterpart
  here.
