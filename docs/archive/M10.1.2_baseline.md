# M10.1.2 the baseline the kernel share is measured against

The `bench` image of every one-core QEMU bench preset and of the three M8.12 reference boards,
on the tree M10.1.1 closed on. The captures were taken at "Record the q35 address-space cost
and what it leaves", "Measure the q35 address-space cost in one session" and "Stop offering the
build's internal cache to Kconfig", and no commit between those three changes an image. Every
figure is the bench's own report in nanoseconds, and the [raw captures](M10.1.2_baseline_captures)
are the whole serial output of each run: ten per QEMU preset, three per board, through
`tools/bench/bench.sh`. The board captures are archived with CRLF normalised to LF, as M9.9's
were, and with the five bytes above 0x7F they held -- one per capture on the F411 and the
ESP32-C6, line noise as the board reset -- written as `?`;
[`M10.1.2_baseline_captures.sha256`](M10.1.2_baseline_captures.sha256) digests the archived
bytes.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| same-task ping-pong, per switch | 1,951 | 5,952 | 6,069 | 8,829 | 21,977 | 36,275 | 17,933 |
| same-task call/reply, 16 B | 9,741 | 19,244 | 31,688 | 15,228 | 52,962 | 82,425 | 38,507 |
| cross-task ping-pong, per switch | 7,507 | 13,553 | 12,220 | 8,834 | 21,818 | 36,307 | 17,920 |
| cross-task call/reply, 16 B | 24,466 | 40,819 | 38,025 | 15,230 | 52,959 | 82,423 | 38,507 |

| task spawn/exit | 86,458 | 110,401 | 132,827 | 31,126 | 156,473 | 255,544 | 173,498 |

A QEMU cell is the median of ten boots; TCG counts work rather than cycles and times it against
the host clock, so a QEMU figure is compared only with one taken in the same session, before and
after interleaved (`docs/archive/M10.1.1_bench.md` shows a row moving by a third between two
sessions on unchanged code). A silicon cell repeated to the nanosecond in all three captures of
its board. On the region boards and on rv32 a cross-task row equals its same-task twin, a task
there being no space of its own.

**What the captures needed.** The capture window `tools/bench/bench-capture.sh` gives an
ST-Link board (25 s) or an Espressif one (40 s) no longer holds the report since M10.1.1 added
its rows: every F411 and ESP32-C6 capture at the defaults stopped short and was refused, and each
completed with `CAP_SECS=120`. The window ends on a clock rather than on `bench: done`, so the
next longer report will outgrow any figure given here. The XMC's cycle counter read dead in two
of its three captures, the condition M8.12 recorded; the rows above are wall-clock and stand.

**Static figures**, the ones the later parts can move:

| | armv6m (micro:bit) | armv7m (Blue Pill) | rv32 | armv8a, rv64 | x86_64 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `sizeof(Thread)` | 280 | 272 | 280 | 496 | 480 |
| `sizeof(Task)` | 12 | 12 | 12 | 16 | 16 |
| `sizeof(Endpoint)` | 24 | 24 | 24 | 48 | 48 |

`hello` is 37,108 bytes of text, 132 of data and 11,000 of bss on the micro:bit, linked against
the nano newlib the board ships, and 33,536, 568 and 7,904 on the Blue Pill against the full
one. The micro:bit's flash and static RAM, 37,240 and 11,132 bytes, are the nano figures
`docs/archive/M9.8_nano.md` recorded.
